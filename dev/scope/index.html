Scope and prior art

What the package does not claim

  • Optimisation does not compose in general. Evaluation composes: the kernel and expected utility of a composite pipeline are the composite of those of its parts (evaluate). Compiled programmes compose: the programme of a chain is the programmes of its parts with shared interface variables (strategy_program). The solution does not: the best policy of a part depends on what the rest of the diagram does with its outputs, and there is no operation here that optimises parts separately and composes the results. CategoricalInfluenceDiagrams documents the obstruction and offers contextual_optimize, which optimises a part in an explicit context; this package reuses it in its tests and does not assert a valuation functor.
  • No string diagrams for credal kernels. The credal monad is not commutative, so its Kleisli category has no tensor product. Credal kernels compose in sequence only (kleisli_compose, CredalMonad). Sequential composition is exactly functorial for the lower pullback; it is loose relative to a model in which one unknown kernel acts at every intermediate value, because the composite lets the second step choose again after every outcome of the first. A DecisionPipeline takes a credal belief at a belief box (and hands it to the robust rules of DecisionAnalysis); its channel and consequence boxes are ordinary stochastic kernels.
  • Backward induction is not offered for every rule. It is optimal for the criterion applied to the whole problem only when the criterion is an Eilenberg-Moore algebra and monotone. For a monotone criterion that is not an algebra (CVaR) it solves the nested rule, a different rule, and says so; for minimax regret it is a labelled heuristic (backward_induction_class).
  • Set-valued products of selection functions are offered for deterministic problems only (optimal_plays), where they were tested against exhaustive search.
  • Optics and open games are not in this package; CategoricalInfluenceDiagrams has an open-game layer.
  • The fidelity bound is usually far from tight. It is a guarantee, not an estimate.
  • Nothing here makes a value of information cheaper to compute or validates a model's causal or calibration claims.

The constructions are known ones. The package implements them, tests them against the core and each other, and backs them with the Lean development in proofs/.

Neither DecisionProgramming.jl nor AlgebraicOptimization.jl is a dependency: the path formulation and the gluing of programmes along interface variables are written here for decision pipelines, following those sources.

Not found by the literature search

Stated as "not found", not "does not exist" (the search of 2025 and 2026 preprints was limited): a categorical or machine-checked treatment of EVPI, EVPPI and EVSI as interchange gaps; a categorical treatment of Savage regret under coarsening (regret_coarsening); a classified list of which decision rules admit backward induction (backward_induction_class); a finite machine-checked additive fidelity bound including tensor.

Not implemented

  • Pareto-front composition of open pipelines (the one setting in which optimisation is known to compose). A two-component finite statement would be: each open component exports the Pareto front of its vectors of boundary values (one coordinate per joint value of its output interface, for each of its policies), and the front of a serial composite is computed from the fronts of the parts. Not attempted here or in Lean.
  • A Wasserstein variant of the fidelity bound for Lipschitz utilities on a metric state space.
  • Graded credal kernels, which would restore a tensor for imprecise kernels.
  • A fidelity certificate for a general pipeline: the schema covers chains on one state space.
